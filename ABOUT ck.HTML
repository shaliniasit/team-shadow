About Us!

Core Functions


1. Diagnosis and Treatment: Using medical technology and clinical staff to find and cure illnesses or injuries.


2. Emergency Care: Operating 24/7 emergency departments for urgent health crises
like accidents or severe trauma.


3. Inpatient and Outpatient Care: Housing patients who need overnight stays or providing same-day treatment and minor procedures


4. Training and Research: Acting as centers for medical education, nursing training, and clinical research


5. Public Health: Supporting community wellness through immunizations and hygiene education



The following types of doctors are available here.


1.(Brain Doctors) Neurologist: A medical doctor who diagnoses and treats disorders of the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system (such as strokes, Alzheimer's, or migraines) using medications and therapies.


2.(Heart Doctors) Cardiologist: A physician who specializes in diagnosing and treating diseases of the heart and blood vessels, such as heart failure or high blood pressure.


3.(Dentist) Dentist: A healthcare professional specialized in the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of conditions affecting the teeth, gums, and mouth.


4.(Skin Doctor) Dermatologist: A medical doctor who specializes in conditions affecting the skin, hair, and nails, ranging from acne and eczema to skin cancer.


5.(Eye Doctor) Ophthalmologist: A medical doctor who specializes in eye and vision care. They can prescribe medication, perform eye surgeries, and fit eyeglasses or contact lenses.


6.(Bone Doctor) Orthopedic Surgeon / Orthopedist: A doctor who specializes in treating the musculoskeletal system, which includes bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and muscles. They treat fractures, sports injuries, and degenerative diseases like arthritis.
